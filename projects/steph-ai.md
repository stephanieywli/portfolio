A personal interest project inspired by [mindbank.ai](https://www.mindbank.ai/).

##How it all started
Call it narcissism or divine inspiration, but steph-ai is a personal interest project inspired by the folks at [mindbank.ai](https://www.mindbank.ai/), who introduced me to the idea of making an AI version of myself. While their goals (i.e., to change humanity) are certainly ambitious, mine were to just have fun and tinker a little to learn more about GPT and NLP models.

##My working process
I started this project by familiarizing myself with OpenAI’s API, by reading through their [quickstart guide](https://platform.openai.com/docs/quickstart) as well as the [docs](https://platform.openai.com/docs/overview). They offered multiple various GPT models, of which I selected [GPT-3.5 Turbo](https://platform.openai.com/docs/models) due to its fast, but mostly inexpensive character.

Next, I researched how to fine-tune the model in a way that was both efficient and relatively accurate. I quickly learned that in order to have quality outputs, I would need quality inputs. Therefore, the next stage of the process was also the longest stage: collecting data about myself and my behavior, in order to fine-tune the model.

I built a simple website scraper using Selenium to collect [200 Deep Questions to Ask If You Really Want to Get to Know Someone](https://thoughtcatalog.com/rania-naim/2016/02/40-deep-questions-to-ask-if-you-really-want-to-get-to-know-someone/), which I am currently manually answering. Although ChatGPT states that only 40 quality inputs are typically required, I felt that for such a personal bot, a few more couldn’t hurt.

##When she'll launch
As I am currently working through the data collection stage, please stay tuned as I plan on finishing these by June, at which point, I will fine-tune and test the accuracy of my model. steph-ai will be available for use on this site, by July 2024 latest. Please come back and talk to steph-ai then!
